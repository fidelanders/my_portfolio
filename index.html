TC‑00 / PROFILE

Quality is a discipline, not a phase.

I'm a QA Engineer at AXA Mansard Plc in Lagos, with five-plus years across fintech and insurance platforms. My work sits at the intersection of manual rigor and automation: designing test strategy, building API and UI automation suites, and running defect lifecycles that hold up under audit.

I've automated with Katalon Studio, Playwright, and Postman/Newman, documented QA processes that survive team turnover, and — more recently — started building AI-augmented tooling that lets test agents reason about a journey's goal instead of following a brittle fixed script.

I hold an MBA in AI from Nexford University, write about QA, AI, and security under The Fifth Layer, and I'm working toward a longer-term move into venture, evaluating startups the way I'd audit a codebase — from the engineering up.
